# 📲Install MonkeyJOB App

Let MonkeyJob be part of your desktop:

---

## 📱iOS

1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button — square with an arrow one.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm — icon lands on your home screen, like any other app.

---

## 💻macOS

**🧭Safari**
1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com).

2. Menu bar → **File → Add to Dock**.
   
   ↳ Or share icon → Add to Dock.

3. MonkeyJob appears in your Dock as a standalone app.

**🌈Chrome**
1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋮ menu → Cast, Save, and Share → Install MonkeyJob.

3. Find it in **Launchpad**, or drag it into your Dock.

---

## 🤖Android

1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com) in Chrome.

2. Tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app**.

   ↳ Tap **Install** if Chrome shows a banner automatically.

3. Confirm — MonkeyJob is added to your home screen and app drawer.

---

## 🪟Windows

**Edge** — best support
1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋯ menu → Apps → Install this site as an app.

3. MonkeyJob opens in its own window and is added to Start Menu.

**🌈Chrome**
1. Open [www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com](https://www.jack-s-onlineblackjack.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋮ menu → Cast, Save, and Share → Install MonkeyJob.

3. Find it in Start Menu, or pin it to your taskbar.
